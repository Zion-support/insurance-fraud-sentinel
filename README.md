# Insurance Fraud Sentinel

**Cross-claim anomaly detection, relationship-network analysis and SIU referral packs.** Part of the [Zion AI App Network](https://ziontechgroup.com/apps/network.html) (Batch 101 — Insurance & Risk AI) by [Zion Tech Group](https://ziontechgroup.com/).

## What it does
- **Cross-claim anomaly detection** — spot repeat claimants, staged-loss patterns and provider rings across your book.
- **Network analysis** — map relationships between claimants, providers, vehicles, addresses and accounts.
- **SIU referral packs** — evidence-ready referrals with timelines, scores and source documents.
- **Intake integration** — receives early fraud flags from [Claims Triage Copilot](https://github.com/Zion-support/claims-triage-copilot) at FNOL.
